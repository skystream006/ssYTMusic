## Gaps and consultation

Run only the relevant device checks in [lifecycle](/home/runner/work/ssYTMusic/ssYTMusic/Skills/playback/references/lifecycle.md) or [presentation](/home/runner/work/ssYTMusic/ssYTMusic/Skills/playback/references/presentation.md): notification pause/resume/stop and focus handoff, six-song background refresh, or real swipe/compact/thumbnail geometry. JVM success alone cannot establish these behaviors.

If media commands or controls affect restrictions, consult [kid-mode](/home/runner/work/ssYTMusic/ssYTMusic/Skills/kid-mode/SKILL.md) and select its policy checks. General branding, settings layout, build, and integration testing are excluded; route those through [repo-router](/home/runner/work/ssYTMusic/ssYTMusic/Skills/repo-router/SKILL.md).
